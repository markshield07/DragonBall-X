DRAGON BALL X

Train hard. Unlock your power. Become a Super Saiyan.

Controls

Meta Quest

  • Punch: physically punch with either hand
  • Trigger: fire the equipped attack (Ki Blast: tap or hold to auto-fire; Charged Blast: hold, then release)
  • Both grips: power up (charges ki)
  • A: transform / power down
  • B: cycle equipped attack
  • Left grip: block
  • X: training menu, Y: comfort options
  • Left stick: move, Right stick: turn

Keyboard & mouse

  • Click canvas to lock the mouse, mouse to look
  • Left click: punch (alternating hands)
  • Right click: fire the equipped attack (Charged Blast: hold, then release)
  • Space (hold): power up (refills ki)
  • T: transform / power down
  • E: cycle attack, Q (hold): block
  • WASD: move, Tab or M: menu
